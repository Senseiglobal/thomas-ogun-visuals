Evidence Archive

Drawing as a practiceof attention.

This selected archive documents 33 pencil studies developed through observation, imagination and symbolic research. The drawings move between portraits, figures, animals, masks, mythic forms and pattern-led compositions.

Working in graphite slows the image down. It gives me space to study gesture, expression and cultural reference before translating those observations into colour, digital illustration, moving image or augmented reality. The page becomes both a place of invention and a record of thought.

What the drawings hold

  • IdentityFigures carry questions of selfhood, ancestry and belonging.
  • SpiritualityMythic forms and symbols keep the unseen present within the image.
  • MemoryRepeated motifs preserve fragments of stories, people and imagined histories.
  • TranslationHandmade studies become a foundation for the wider interdisciplinary practice.
